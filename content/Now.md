As an educator I work with children, young adults, teachers, learning facilitators, and researchers on a daily basis. Apart from that I write, I paint, and sometimes I perform. I love writing [weekly notes](/tags/weekly-notes) and [blog posts](/tags/blog/) here.

## Work

**Core team member / [TIDE Foundation](https://tideinternational.org/)**, Ahmedabad

We are a group of passionate educators, researchers & volunteers facilitating transformational learning experiences with children and young adults grounded in the philosophy of - **'Education for Harmony'**.

### Side Projects

*   Gujarati children's literature magazine - **Patrango** (Team: Mihir, Bansi)
*   Puppet show on Kharai Camel - **Rewilding Storytelling Fellowship**
