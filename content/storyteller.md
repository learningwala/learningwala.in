### I write, perform and tell stories. **Sharing narrative** is how I express myself and make meaning of this world.

---

- I write poems, essays and stories, mostly in my mother tongue, Gujarati. Sometimes I experiment with mediums like story‘telling’, theater and puppets.

- Through my non-fiction work, I share my experiences of working with children, pedagogical experiments and educational philosophy.

*This page is a living portfolio of my creative practice as a writer, performer, and maker.*
