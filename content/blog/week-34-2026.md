**My inquiry :**


I have been working with children for the last 13 years now. I have tried different pedagogies (nature exploration, theatre, project-based learning), and worked with children from different socio-economic backgrounds, in both formal and informal setups. During this journey, the answers to the following questions have evolved:

- Why am I working with children?
- What is education for me / what is the aim of education?

I have documented these through notes, blog posts, and articles. I now want to document them more formally through some medium.

Apart from that, I would like to take up the following inquiry:

How can one be 'sahaj' while teaching or facilitating in the classroom? How does one step out of the role of "teacher," not use one's power, and instead be present and sensitive? I feel that learning happens in relationships — the relationship between teacher and student, with oneself, and with the more-than-human world. To create a dialogic situation in the classroom, the teacher needs to drop his/her agendas and be present to whatever is happening in that moment, without biases or assumptions.

How can an educator be sahaj — the same person inside the classroom as they are outside it? How can he/she quiet the chatter of the 'mana,' and from that sahaj sthiti, that sensitive presence, respond to the children around them?

How as an educator I take one craft or art as a practice to be sahaj ?

let me try to put more clearly : **what qualities you need to develop to create dialogic learning environment** or **how do you make authentic connection with the person / learner**
