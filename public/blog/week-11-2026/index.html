Week 11/2026

2026-03-16 · Mihir Pathak

9th to 15th March 2026 (Mon to Sunday)

Ah, it is difficult to sit and write this week’s note. Too many thoughts are going on.

The non-profit registration process is stretching a little — back and forth is going on. Hopefully it will be done soon.

The children’s literature project is also moving at its own pace. I am not controlling anything this time. I am trying to learn how a project can flourish on its own pace.

I am planning to set up Khojbeen Mandali’s website and email. Searching for open-source, low-tech alternatives for the same.

Summer is here. The temperature and light are reminding me of vacations, children’s play, sleep, mangoes — and something familiar, though I don’t know exactly what…

Me and Nidhi started watching an old TV series called Dil Mil Gaye. For no reason really — it is just cute.

It’s okay to watch mindless stuff sometimes. We actually found something which we can watch together.
