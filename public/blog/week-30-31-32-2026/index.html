2026-08-09 · Mihir Pathak

20th July to 9th Aug 2026

Highlights from last three weeks :

  • Jantar mantar protest. What is my stand - I support descent as a process. But I do not support violence happening because of entrance exams and mono culture in education. I encourage re-imagining the education system at large, what if communities design their own curriculum, according to their local - learning needs instead of a centralized curriculum. Read article by Yvan Aves on ‘curriculum for belongingness’

  • I got selected for a fellowship called ‘rewilding storytelling’. I will be traveling to coonoor in the last week of august for the fellowship residency

  • I visited vadodara. Along with other work I met Vocies of Vishwamitri group working on Vishwamitri river. I am trying work with them to make comprehensive website about the cause.

  • I met two young girls in vadodara, they started brand new NGO and they working with children at my home town Jaspur. This not an org started by rich person to started with ‘giving back’ mindset. It is started by people like me and you, who are still struggling to make ends meet but still want to do something in social sector.

  • I took session at MS University, Vadodara.
