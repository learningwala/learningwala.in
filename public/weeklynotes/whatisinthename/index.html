What is in the name?

2026-11-22 · Mihir Pathak

This post is inspired by prompt (pirated) from indie web blr meetup.

In this post i would like to share story behind my internet username and my personal website domain name - learningwala

I guess around 2015 I discovered the work of filmmaker anand gandhi. I watched his film ‘ship of theseus’, later on I watched his TED Talk and almost all the interviews available on youtube. I really liked his work and thesis - how he is mixing evolutionary biology, philosophy and storytelling. I stalked him on twitter where his username is ‘memewala’ coming from a theory he keeps mentioning in his talks ‘memetics’ (Richard Dawkins - Selfish gene).

I got really inspired by this idea. I started thinking about what should be my username which reflects my work and philosophy ? I am in the education sector so ‘learning’ came naturally to me. So I changed or made a new account (I don’t remember) with the username - learningwala. Later I made email id learningwalamihir@gmail.com (because only learningwala was not available)

Slowly all my internet identity become learningwala - be it instagram, linkedin, github, medium or any other platform. I started using learningwala email id very recently - 2022 when my other email id’s google drive got full. Now this one have paid plan.

Before learningwala.in I had bought learningwala.com , that time I was using wix based website. I made mistake of buying domain name from wix. One day because of some debit card issue I couldn’t renew learningwala.com and I lost it. Immediately someone started hosting e-learning site on that domain. (I am checking right now while I am writing, it is not reachable). After this tragedy I bought learningwala.in from godaddy. I think it’s three years now. I recently renewed it for one more year. Only for one year because I didn’t have money in my account + I wanted to think if I want to shift to mihirpathak.xyz or should I continue with learningwala.in

Learningwala is brand name kind of thing in my circle, so I am afraid to change it but sometimes I feel that it is limiting me and I should have domain name of my personal website with my full name.

Let me tell you story of my old wordpress based blog - https://mihirism.wordpress.com/ It is very very funny to read my thoughts from decade ago. I was inspired by name of famous gujarati blog from Kartik Mistry - kartikm.wordpress.com , to make it ‘m’ at the end and give some twist I made my blog name ‘mihirism’ - finding swadharama of mihir which is mihirism.

I am feeling very embarrassed while reading my old posts from that blog. But anyway.. People are always ‘work in progress’.
