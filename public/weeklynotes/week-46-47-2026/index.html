Week 46 - 47 /2026

2026-11-22 · Mihir Pathak

9th to 22th Nov 2026

Oh, it was a super busy two weeks.

I am feeling burnt out because of constant work and the stress of conflicts at home. I am not able to concentrate on work. I keep distracting myself by doing different things: reading, editing the website.

I am seriously thinking of taking a break, a short sabbatical of sorts, so I can rejuvenate. I want my mind to be free from most responsibilities and pressure, at least for some time. I want to eat good food, spend time with N, read without an agenda, go for walks, be in one place (I am feeling rushed and restless), and meet old friends.

I am also feeling like doing a course at a university, just to have pressure-free time to follow my curiosity and discuss ideas with peers. I tried designing a self-learning journey with a specific mentor, but it is not working out.

I do not have the financial backup to take a long sabbatical. Even figuring out a one-month break is a big deal for me. I would need to ask friends for help. I was thinking about getting a fellowship or something similar, but that would again bring pressure and deliverables.

Last two weeks

9th to 15th Nov

I was at home until the 11th because of the Diwali vacation. From the 12th, our office reopened, and we started the day with a little puja, best wishes, and intention setting.

B came over, and we stayed up till late at night. We did some work on Patrango and also planned a writers’ retreat for December.

N and I made a trailer video of our puppet show to submit to a storytelling fellowship. I am not fully satisfied with the story, but we needed to deliver something before the deadline. We can improve it later.

On Gujarati New Year’s Day, I went to find a lawyer to finish some admin work for Eklavya. Thankfully, we found one. It was an interesting experience.

I also made lots of small tweaks to the website.

16th to 22nd Nov

A colleague from the office and I conducted a workshop with teachers of Grades 9 to 12 from a school near Halol. After a long time, I took a demo session on Gujarati language, and I was very happy with how it went. The teachers were a little rigid and resistant to trying new things, but I am glad I gave my best. I also understand their limitations.

After that, I conducted a session with early childhood learning centre facilitators from UBS Foundation, Ahmedabad. We talked about children’s literature, language, the affective domain, and the development of the senses. We also made puppets from waste material for storytelling. Insta reel on two days workshop

The highlight of the workshop was reading and discussing a Hindi article on how stories, language, and adults’ behaviour shape a child’s relationship with the living world. The article was recently published in Sandarbh magazine. The facilitators understood it and reflected on its argument. That was the highlight for me, because these teachers come from marginalised backgrounds and only a few have finished graduation. Reading, relating, and reflecting is a big thing for them. Overall, I am very happy about the workshop.

Mitali ben was in town for a teacher training workshop at the community education centre. She stayed with us for two days. I was tired and busy with the UBS workshop, so I couldn’t interact much, but Nidhi enjoyed it a lot. We also met Jwalin.

I had a check-in call with Srishti after a long time. She shared about the teacher fellowship she is running, as well as her recent research paper.

I attended the ASSITEJ India online gathering on Theatre for Early Years. It was about forming a group or network of people interested in theatre for early years and babies.
