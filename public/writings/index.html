Storyteller

I like writing, theater and multi sensorial storytelling. Creating and sharing narrative is process of making sense or creating meaning of this world.

Through my fiction work I share stories of day to day wonder, relationships, inner movements and more. Through my non fiction work I share my experiences of working with children, pedagogical experiments and educational philosophy.
