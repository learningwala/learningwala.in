MihirPathak

Storyteller{ Writing · Theater · Multi sensorial storytelling }

I am interested in writing, theater and multi sensorial storytelling.

I have self-published two books in Gujarati, and I am co-publishing a children’s magazine called Patrango.

I try to find and share narratives about day to day wonder, human-nature relationships, interconnections, human desire, ambition, inner conflicts, harmony and more.

In my non-fiction work, I write about my experiences with children, pedagogical experiments, education philosophy and more.
