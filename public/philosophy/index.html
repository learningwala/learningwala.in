As a society, we are undergoing an interconnected meta-crisis across personal, social, and ecological spheres. At its deepest level, I believe this crisis to be a crisis of consciousness. How can we understand ourselves, experience our interconnectedness with the world, and prepare to act with care, keeping everyone’s wellbeing in mind, so that we live in harmony with existence? What does the educational process look like if our collective purpose is to live together harmoniously?

I believe that the core of all of this is our changed relationships / fragmentation. And modern education is one of the biggest influencer in all of others.

Irrelationships caused by modern education :

  • Disconnection from oneself
  • Disconnection from community and society
  • Disconnection from Earth / biosphere / land
  • Disconnection between subjects and disciplines
  • Disconnection between “learning” and real-world engagement

in my daily living I observe, reflect, question my relationship with family, land, water, career, money, waste, everything… it is daily quest to be in harmony. the underlying search is how to live with sensitivity, authenticity in this very interconnected world.. how to act and live so it is for well-being of self, community and environment.

Is there a different way of living, both in personal and in social terms, that is not conflict ridden but essentially peaceful and whole? What role does education and creative practice have in this process?

I am primarily interested in creative practice, but I didn’t find space in mainstream arts community, instead of that I find my space with children and young adults. I like being with them and exploring different creative processes with them. slowly by reading, reflecting and embodied experiences my philosophy of education emerged where I try to find pedagogues which explore the aim of education as ’education for harmony’. Similarly I do writing, storytelling / performance as my creative practice. there also I try to find and share narratives about day to day wonder, human - nature relationships, interconnections, human desire, ambition, inner conflicts and more. Most of the time my creative practice intertwined with my educational work.

For me work in education and working on my creative practices is process of practicing alivlihood

Rewilding education :

Learning should be dialogic, reciprocal and inquiry based: The teacher is not the sole authority or a transmitter of knowledge and learning happens when teacher and students go through the process of questioning, sharing, listening, and exploring together. Learning does not happen through teaching; it happens through meeting, the meeting of I and Thou (Martin Buber).

Learning should be relational, contextual, interdisciplinary: Learning involves building deeper relationships - with oneself, with others and the wider society, with place, and the more-than-human world.

Learning should be experiential, embodied and action based: Learning should happen with the whole being of an individual, engaging the head, heart, hand and soul. Experiments, projects, yatras, activities, inquiries, films, music can create opportunities for learning to be contextual, experienced, felt and lived.

My understanding of education is coming from humanist, existential , experiential linage

Education is the practice of meeting—relating authentically with—ourselves, one another, our places, and the more-than-human world, through looking, listening, questioning, reflecting, and creating together.

Learning with head, heart, hands, and soul, we deepen our understanding of ourselves and our interconnections, cultivating the sensitivity, critical discernment, and courage to act freely, responsibly, and with care in a shared world.

How to facilitate this type of education ?

Do you wish to be a genuine teacher? Here is your responsibility: Enter into the being of the person in your student. Accept him/her unconditionally. Create a space around by enabling him to see and remove the pressure of assumptions & beliefs. Encourage to risk flights in the unknown, seamless life. Reflect together on the learning-unlearning from that flight. Enjoy the friendship. - Mahendra Chotalia
