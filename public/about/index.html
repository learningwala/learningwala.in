About

Hi, this is Mihir Pathak, aka learningwala.

I have not been to a formal school since completing 10th grade. Later, I completed 12th grade and a BA in Sociology as an external student. I have been working with children since the age of 18.

Instead of going to school, I spent time exploring website and software development, sales and marketing, theater, writing, design, technology, entrepreneurship and many more interests.

⁂

How it began

I started working with children by sharing how to use a computer, inspired by the Hole in the Wall project by Sugata Mitra. I explored ‘MIT Scratch’, made Arvind Gupta toys, told stories, played games and more.

Slowly, after lots of experimentation, I realized that:

  • I like being with children and young adults. I can be myself.
  • I can do creative experiments and explorations with them, which I can’t do in school or university.

I started volunteering in different schools, meeting inspiring, passionate educators and children, visiting different learning spaces across India, reading books and attending workshops. That is how my journey as an educator started, and it is evolving every day.

During this decade long journey, I have worked in formal and informal learning spaces across India, and made friends with many children and educators.

⁂

What I do

I create transformative learning journeys for children, young adults and educators using experiential methodologies, including theater, nature-based explorations and interdisciplinary projects.

My programs, workshops and interventions are just an anchor. My strong intention is to create a space of dialogue and co-creation.

A space which acts as an incubation center for everyone, where we learn, experiment, challenge ourselves, and thereby break barriers and grow in our lives.

My current core interest areas

  • Understanding the role of love and relationship in education
  • Project based learning, self directed learning and non formal learning spaces
  • Child development and learning
  • Language pedagogy, children’s literature and theater in education
  • Life skills education and ecological consciousness / nature education
  • Citizen science projects, environment, civics, cultural projects for the larger good

My work is deeply inspired by the philosophies of J. Krishnamurti and Martin Buber, and by the science of the brain, learning and child development.

⁂

Right now

I am working with formal and informal learning environments in different capacities, directly with children, young adults, educators, researchers and learning experience designers. You can read about my current engagements on the now page.

I am part of the faculty team for the ‘Child Development, Learning & Special Needs’ course at Eklavya Foundation, Bhopal. I am also a proud recipient of the Earth Educator Fellowship (2022-23), supported by the Youth Conservation Action Network.

⁂

Outside work

As an amateur writer, I enjoy crafting poetry and aspire to write stories, poems and plays. So far, I have self-published two books in Gujarati.

When I am not with children or educators, I immerse myself in writing poetry or documenting my ’learning experiments’.

Music, long walks, bird watching, singing and sketching are my favorite time accelerators.
